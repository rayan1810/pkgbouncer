# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version is below.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to security tooling
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear
- Own your mistakes and learn from them

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information without permission
- Spam, or using the issue tracker to promote unrelated products
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider harmful.

Report problems by email to rohit@tria.so. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The full enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 apply.
